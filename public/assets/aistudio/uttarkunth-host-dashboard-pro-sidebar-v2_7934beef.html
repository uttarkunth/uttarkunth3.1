Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common host questions
Do pending booking requests block my room dates?

No. Pending requests do not block room availability. Dates become reserved only after the host confirms the request, subject to a server-side availability check.

How do guests pay for their stay?

Under the current model, guests pay hosts directly at the property. Uttarkunth does not collect online payments.

Can I close dates for maintenance or personal use?

Yes. Use Availability Calendar to create a manual block for a particular room and date range. A block must not overlap a confirmed reservation.
